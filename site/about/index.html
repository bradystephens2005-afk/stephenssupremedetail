A small shop in Greenville, South Carolina

Perch & Purr is run by one person. I'm Brady.

I work construction Monday through Thursday and run this shop the rest of the week. Building things for a living makes me picky about what holds weight and how it's installed, and that's how I chose what to sell.

The Cordless Window Perch is the heart of the shop. It skips the dangling cables most window hammocks use. It holds on with four suction cups and a stainless steel frame, goes up in about a minute, and folds flat when you need the window back.

I don't make the perch myself. My job is choosing products carefully, describing them honestly, getting them to you quickly, and standing behind them with a 30-day return window.
