Fit and safety

Where it fits

  • Smooth, flat glass with room for all four cups. Double-pane is fine.
  • Not for textured or frosted glass, window film, screens, wood or painted surfaces.
  • A pane that doesn't open is best. If it opens, keep it shut while the perch is up.

Keeping it safe

  • Press down hard on the bed with your hand before your cat's first jump.
  • Mount it at a height your cat can reach and get down from easily.
  • Check the cups once a week, and after big temperature swings at the window.
  • Tested to 40 lb on clean glass. Keep the total weight on it, cats included, under that.
  • Watch the first few visits until you're both sure of it.

Questions

Will it really hold my cat?

The frame is painted stainless steel and the perch is tested to 40 lb, which covers almost every house cat. That number comes from a load test on clean, smooth glass. How well suction cups hold in your home depends on your glass and how they're pressed on, so always press down on the perch with your hand before your cat uses it.

What kind of window does it need?

Smooth, flat glass with room for all four cups. Double-pane windows are fine. It won't hold on textured or frosted glass, window film, screens, or wood and painted surfaces. A pane that doesn't open is best. If it does open, keep that window shut while the perch is up.

What if a suction cup lets go?

It's usually dust, dry cups, or a big temperature swing at the window. Take the cup off, rinse it in warm water, clean the glass, and press it back on. Press down on the perch once a week to check the cups.

How do I wash the cover?

Unzip it from the frame and wash it as the care tag says. Let it dry all the way before zipping it back on. The bundle comes with a spare fleece pad, so your cat still has a seat on laundry day.

How does the Fur Lifter work?

Run it back and forth over couches, bedding or a cat tree and it lifts the loose fur. It's reusable, so there are no sticky sheets or refills to buy.
