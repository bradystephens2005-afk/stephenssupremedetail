Shipping

Last updated October 2026

  • Where: the 48 contiguous US states. We don't ship to Alaska, Hawaii, US territories, military addresses or outside the US yet.
  • When: orders are processed within 3 business days (Monday to Friday, not counting US holidays). Transit takes 3 to 7 business days after that.
  • Tracking: we email you a tracking number when your order ships.
  • Cost: the Cordless Window Perch and The Cozy Window Bundle ship free. The Reusable Fur Lifter ships for $5.99.
  • More than one product: each product checks out on its own Stripe page, so the Fur Lifter's shipping is charged on its checkout.

If your order arrives damaged, or tracking says it was delivered and you can't find it, email us within 7 days of the delivery date with your order email and, if damaged, a photo. We'll work with you and the carrier to replace it or refund it.

Returns

Last updated October 2026

You have 30 days from delivery to return anything you bought from us.

  1. Email perchandpurr@gmail.com with the email you used at checkout and a line about what's going on. We reply within one business day with the return address.
  2. Pack up all the parts (perch, cups, cover, and anything else that came with it), cleaned of cat hair if you can.
  3. Ship it back and send us the tracking number.
  • Refunds go back to your original payment method through Stripe within 5 business days of the return reaching us. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.
  • Return shipping: if the item arrived damaged, defective or wrong, we pay for return shipping. For other returns, return shipping is up to you.
  • Exchanges: return the item and place a new order.

Privacy

Last updated October 2026

What we collect. When you buy, Stripe collects your name, email, shipping address and payment details on its secure checkout page. We receive your name, email, address and what you ordered. We never see your full card number. If you join the launch list or leave a review, we get what you type into that form.

What stays in your browser. Your cart is saved in your own browser (local storage) so it's still there when you change pages. It isn't sent to us. This site has no advertising trackers and no analytics cookies.

How we use it. To fill and ship your order, send tracking, answer your emails, and send launch-list emails if you signed up. We don't sell or rent your information.

Who else handles it. Stripe (payments and sales tax), Netlify (hosting this site and receiving form entries), and the shipping carrier and supplier that get your order to you, who see only your name and address.

Your choices. Email perchandpurr@gmail.com to see, correct or delete what we have about you, or to leave the launch list.

Last updated October 2026

  • Prices are in US dollars. Sales tax is added at checkout where required.

  • As far as the law allows, our responsibility for any order is limited to what you paid for it.
