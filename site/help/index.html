Setup guide

About a minute, no tools.

1. Pick the pane

Choose smooth, flat glass with room for all four cups, at a height your cat can reach and get down from easily. A pane that doesn't open is best.

2. Clean the glass and the cups

Wipe the glass with glass cleaner or rubbing alcohol and let it dry. Rinse the suction cups in warm water so they're soft and free of dust.

3. Press each cup on

Unfold the perch and hold it level. Press the top cups on first, then the bottom ones, pushing each flat until the air is out.

4. Press-test, then invite your cat

Push down hard on the bed with your hand. If nothing shifts, zip on the cover, fleece side up for cold weather or mesh side up for warm weather, and let your cat take it from there.

Once a week

Press down on the bed to check the cups. Re-seat any cup that has lifted at the edge. Check again after a big change in weather, since cold glass and dry air are hard on suction cups.

If a cup slips

  1. Take the perch down.
  2. Rinse each cup in warm water and dry it. Warm cups are softer and seal better.
  3. Clean the glass again with rubbing alcohol. Hand lotion, dust and cleaning residue all break the seal.
  4. Press the cups back on, top ones first, and press-test before your cat uses it.

Still slipping? The glass may be textured or coated. Email us and we'll help you sort it out, or set up a return.

Caring for the cover

  • Unzip the cover from the frame before washing.
  • Wash it the way the care tag says, and let it dry completely.
  • Flip it with the seasons: fleece up for cold weather, mesh up for warm weather.
  • Wipe the frame with a damp cloth when you need to.

Questions

Will it really hold my cat?

The frame is painted stainless steel and the perch is tested to 40 lb, which covers almost every house cat. That number comes from a load test on clean, smooth glass. How well suction cups hold in your home depends on your glass and how they're pressed on, so always press down on the perch with your hand before your cat uses it.

What kind of window does it need?

Smooth, flat glass with room for all four cups. Double-pane windows are fine. It won't hold on textured or frosted glass, window film, screens, or wood and painted surfaces. A pane that doesn't open is best. If it does open, keep that window shut while the perch is up.

How long does setup take?

About a minute once the glass is clean. Press on the four cups, zip on the cover, and press-test it. The setup guide walks through it.

What if a suction cup lets go?

It's usually dust, dry cups, or a big temperature swing at the window. Take the cup off, rinse it in warm water, clean the glass, and press it back on. Press down on the perch once a week to check the cups.

How do I wash the cover?

Unzip it from the frame and wash it as the care tag says. Let it dry all the way before zipping it back on. The bundle comes with a spare fleece pad, so your cat still has a seat on laundry day.

How does the Fur Lifter work?

Run it back and forth over couches, bedding or a cat tree and it lifts the loose fur. It's reusable, so there are no sticky sheets or refills to buy.

Is the Fur Lifter safe on every fabric?

It's made for upholstery, bedding and other everyday fabrics. On delicate fabrics like silk or velvet, try a hidden spot first. It's not meant for brushing your cat.
